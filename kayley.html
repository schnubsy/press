Going deeper, later

Project management: your options

For the sprint, Option 1 is the pick. Consider the others only if PM becomes your direction after the internship — Dad's PMP means he can mentor you straight toward CAPM.

PM Foundations · LinkedIn LearningRecommended

Pros

  • Free on your account
  • ~3h fits the sprint
  • Recognized beginner standard, PMBOK-aligned
  • LinkedIn-postable certificate

Cons

  • Completion certificate, not a formal credential
  • No exam rigor or formal PMI recognition

Google PM Certificate · Coursera

Pros

  • Strong brand; deep Agile/Scrum content
  • Earns 100+ PMI education hours toward CAPM/PMP
  • Built with PMI input

Cons

  • ~$49/mo (~$294 over ~6 months)
  • Far longer than 2 weeks; overkill for a ramp
  • Not a PMI certification itself

CAPM · PMI certification

Pros

  • Real, globally recognized PMI credential
  • Strongest entry-level employer signal
  • PMBOK-based

Cons

  • Proctored exam + 23 education hours
  • Weeks of prep; ~$225–300 exam fee
  • Genuinely hard for a true beginner — wrong level now
Keep in mind

Notes & gotchas

🆕
Pick the newest version.

LinkedIn often lists 2–3 versions of a course — always choose the most recent release date.

📜
Certificate ≠ certification.

LinkedIn & Anthropic give completion certificates — perfect for a ramp. CAPM is a formal certification for later.

🛠️
Doing beats watching.

If a course feels passive, pause and replicate the exercise in the real app. Retention comes from reps, not runtime.

⚡
AI is a speed boost, not a crutch.

Let Copilot/Claude draft, then make sure you understand and can defend the output yourself.
