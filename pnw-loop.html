Park passes, explained

  • They're parking fees, not road tolls. You only need one when you park at a state park. Driving US-101, OR-6 or the Gorge highway needs nothing.
  • Saturday, Washington: a one-day Discover Pass ($10) to park at Cape Disappointment. Easiest is the pay station at the park. Buying online gives a pass you have to print.
  • Oregon, Sat, Sun and Mon: a day-use parking permit, $12 per car per day for out-of-state plates. One permit covers every Oregon state park that day: Fort Stevens (the shipwreck) on Saturday, Ecola on Sunday, Vista House and Latourell on Monday. Buy it at the yellow machine or by scanning the QR sign in the first lot of the day, or online that day. Keep the receipt on the dash.
  • No pass needed: Multnomah Falls in November, Haystack Rock, the Astoria Column (just $10 parking), Pittock Mansion and the Japanese Garden (city meters).
